Know your number

Test. Measure. Act. Retest.

An omega-3 blood test can provide one measurement to explore in context. It is not a diagnosis, a verdict, or the whole story.

  1. 01Test

    Begin with a verified at-home testing tool when measuring answers a clear question.

  2. 02Measure

    Read the result with its method, limitations, and surrounding context in view.

  3. 03Act

    Choose an informed next step—which may be learning, discussing, changing a habit, or doing nothing yet.

  4. 04Retest

    If appropriate, use another measurement to compare rather than relying on guesswork.
