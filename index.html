Know your number

Test. Measure. Act. Retest.

An omega-3 blood test can provide one measurement to explore in context. It is not a diagnosis, a verdict, or the whole story.

  1. 01Test

    Begin with a verified at-home testing tool when measuring answers a clear question.

  2. 02Measure

    Read the result with its method, limitations, and surrounding context in view.

  3. 03Act

    Choose an informed next step—which may be learning, discussing, changing a habit, or doing nothing yet.

  4. 04Retest

    If appropriate, use another measurement to compare rather than relying on guesswork.

Our standards

You deserve to know why.

Open each principle to inspect the standard behind the signal.

Editorial
integrity
  1. 01We explain our reasoning

    If we recommend something, we tell you why.

  2. 02We show our sources

    Whenever possible, education should link back to credible evidence.

  3. 03We separate evidence from opinion

    What research suggests and what we personally think should not be presented as the same thing.

  4. 04We disclose our partnerships

    If we can financially benefit from something, visitors should know.

  5. 05We tell you when we don't know

    Uncertainty should not be hidden.
