# V10 Library source and duplication audit

Scope: all 10 published Library guides. This audit compares current citations with private thematic research leads without copying private text or treating manufacturer-selected science as independent validation.

## Cross-Library findings

- Every guide has unique source URLs within the guide; no within-article duplicate was found.
- Nine public URLs recur across related guides. The reuse is topically appropriate, but each revision should preserve article-specific interpretation rather than duplicating prose.
- The NIH omega-3 fact sheet appears in three omega/testing guides. Eight other authoritative or peer-reviewed sources appear in two related guides each.
- Current guides consistently include limitations and source sections. No factual emergency requiring automatic copy changes was identified.
- The private audit contains 34 research/reference-only leads. Their strongest themes are omega-3, testing/biomarkers, pregnancy, quality/certification, saffron/mood, skin/collagen, gut/digestion, and vitamin D. None is publication-ready solely because it exists in the private source.
- No published guide cites a V10-private URL or private extracted document.

## Publication decision

No existing guide was rewritten and no new health article was published in V10. The evidence index is a source-discovery layer, not a silent article-update mechanism. Future changes require independent source verification, original writing, limitations, compliance review, and explicit candidate scope.
