# V10 manufacturer-policy gap

This is an evidence-backed manufacturer-policy audit, not legal advice.

## External-site approval — DEFERRED

No written approval for the current external website was found in the authorized public repository state or supplied as user-approved evidence. The reviewed manufacturer rules require prior compliance review/approval for an external personal website, identify material changes as potentially requiring renewed approval, and time-limit approval. V10 therefore does not claim approval.

Required action: obtain written manufacturer/compliance confirmation that explicitly covers the current domain, V10 evidence/source experience, commercial destinations, material presentation, and mixed-brand educational model. Preserve the approval date, approved scope, expiration, and change-review conditions outside public content.

## Independent Partner identification — PARTIAL / DEFERRED

The public site visibly identifies Gavin Reidel as a Zinzino Independent Partner and distinguishes purchases as occurring on the manufacturer site. It does not represent The Mindful Matrix as Zinzino.

The reviewed rules also call for public name/address/contact information in the approved external-site model. The repository contains no user-approved public business address or contact channel. V10 does not mine Git metadata, private records, or account data to invent one.

Required action: approve a business-safe public contact record and confirm with manufacturer compliance exactly which name, address, and contact fields must render. Until then, `content/compliance/public-contact.json` remains `pending_user_approval` with `renderPublicly: false`.

## Mixed Zinzino/BioLimitless platform — DEFERRED MATERIAL CONFLICT

The current site combines Zinzino and BioLimitless product destinations and links to independent government resources. The reviewed Zinzino external-site model restricts third-party goods/services and most non-Zinzino links. That creates a material unresolved policy conflict; partner status alone is not evidence of an exception.

Safe options for user/manufacturer review:

1. Obtain written approval for the current mixed-brand educational platform and its independent-source links.
2. Separate Zinzino commercial presentation onto an approved Zinzino-only surface while retaining a noncommercial educational Matrix surface.
3. Remove or disable Zinzino commercial routing from the mixed-brand surface until approval is documented.
4. Use only the manufacturer-provided replicated shop for Zinzino transactions and keep third-party product promotion elsewhere, subject to written confirmation.

V10 does not choose among these business options, imply approval, or publish private policy text. It completes unrelated safe infrastructure while leaving manufacturer-specific private resources and approval-dependent content disabled.

## Claims and source labeling

The eight V10 public sources are labeled as independent public/government context. They are not presented as manufacturer validation or product efficacy evidence. Product inspectors distinguish product-specific context from department context and state that context does not prove an outcome. Existing health-claim gates remain unchanged and pass.
