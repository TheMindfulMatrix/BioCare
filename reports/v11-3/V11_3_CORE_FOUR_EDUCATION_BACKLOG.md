# V11.3 Core Four Education Backlog

Non-public planning only. Nothing in this backlog is approved for publication or establishes that an individual needs a supplement.

## Vitamin K2 within D3 + K2

- Visitor questions: What distinguishes K1 from K2 and MK forms? What does the label actually identify? Which questions concern vitamin D versus vitamin K? What medication interactions require clinician involvement?
- Existing Matrix coverage: Vitamin D testing and NIH Vitamin D context; general supplement-label literacy.
- Missing coverage: K2-specific independent education, evidence-strength discussion, form-specific context, and anticoagulant interaction boundaries.
- Priority source: NIH ODS Vitamin K Health Professional Fact Sheet — https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/
- Research target: current systematic reviews separating vitamin K forms, populations, outcomes, and co-supplementation.
- Claims to exclude without narrow support: cardiovascular protection, directing calcium, preventing osteoporosis, universal deficiency, or universal need.
- Manufacturer boundary: use documentation only for identity, form, amount, directions, warnings, and other product-specific label facts.
- Suggested outline: terminology → food and supplement forms → intake/status context → evidence by outcome → interactions/safety → uncertainty → reading the product label.
- Review gate: nutrition/medical and compliance review; recheck government guidance and systematic reviews within 12 months of publication.

## Zinc and copper relationship

- Visitor questions: Why are zinc and copper paired? What do intake recommendations and upper limits mean? When can higher zinc intake affect copper status? What does this product label document?
- Existing Matrix coverage: general supplement-label guide and general NIH supplement context.
- Missing coverage: dedicated zinc and copper education and independent product-context mapping.
- Priority sources: NIH ODS Zinc and Copper Health Professional Fact Sheets — https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/ and https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Copper-HealthProfessional/
- Research target: current systematic reviews on zinc–copper interactions, dose/duration context, and population differences.
- Claims to exclude without narrow support: immune treatment, infection prevention, correcting deficiency, or a universally ideal ratio.
- Manufacturer boundary: label facts do not establish efficacy, individual need, or equivalence to ingredient studies.
- Suggested outline: roles and sources → intake/upper-limit context → interaction mechanics → label interpretation → safety/medications → uncertainty.
- Review gate: nutrition/medical and compliance review; source freshness check within 12 months of publication.
## Magnesium and magnesium glycinate

- Visitor questions: What does “glycinate” describe? How do forms differ in label identity and available evidence? What can intake guidance establish? What safety and medication questions matter?
- Existing Matrix coverage: general supplement-label guide and general NIH supplement context.
- Missing coverage: dedicated magnesium education, form-specific evidence review, and independent mapping for the finished product.
- Priority source: NIH ODS Magnesium Health Professional Fact Sheet — https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/
- Research target: systematic reviews comparing oral magnesium forms while separating absorption measures from clinical outcomes.
- Claims to exclude without narrow support: improving sleep, reducing anxiety, guaranteeing recovery, treating cramps, or universal deficiency.
- Manufacturer boundary: documentation may establish the labeled form and amount, but not ingredient-study transfer to the finished product.
- Suggested outline: magnesium basics → intake/status limits → form terminology → evidence by question → safety/interactions → label-reading checklist → uncertainty.
- Review gate: nutrition/medical and compliance review; source freshness check within 12 months of publication.
