The principles behind the pages.

01

Information first

Begin with the question you want to understand. The site connects practical guides, product records, and source material without treating them as interchangeable.

02

Sources with context

Library guides show references and limitations. Manufacturer documentation describes a product; it is not the same as independent evidence about an outcome.

03

An optional next step

Reading more, asking a professional, or doing nothing yet are valid choices. Visiting a product page does not commit you to a purchase.

Editorial transparency

What inclusion means—and what it doesn't.

Products are organized by format, stated purpose, available label information, and connections to the Library. Inclusion reflects an editorial choice. It does not establish effectiveness or suitability for an individual.

Evidence and manufacturer material are identified separately. Missing documentation stays visible; research about one ingredient does not automatically establish a benefit for a finished product.

Source checks and automated validation are not independent clinical review. The website is educational and does not provide personalized medical advice.

Good questions

Before you get in touch.

Who handles my purchase?

Product links take you to the named manufacturer's website. That checkout presents the seller's current price and terms. For orders, delivery, returns, or subscriptions, use the contact details on your order confirmation or the manufacturer's support page.

How does The Mindful Matrix earn money?

Some product links are affiliate or partner links. A qualifying purchase may earn compensation for The Mindful Matrix. The relationship is disclosed alongside commercial content; it does not establish that a product is right for you.

Can you interpret my test results?

The site explains general concepts and source limitations, not individual results. Take personal questions to a qualified healthcare professional; do not email your test report to this website.

Can I read without buying or subscribing?

Yes. The public Library and source index can be read without an account, a purchase, or an email subscription.
