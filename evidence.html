Know the source

Independent context is not manufacturer documentation.

Government fact sheets and clinical guidelines explain broader context. Trials, observational studies, and expert consensus answer different questions and may have funding or author conflicts. Manufacturer material describes the product, not independent validation. None of these source types is a personalized recommendation.

Ingredient/topic connections help you navigate. They do not establish the safety or effectiveness of a finished product. The new Library guides are editorial source checks, not independently clinician-reviewed advice.
