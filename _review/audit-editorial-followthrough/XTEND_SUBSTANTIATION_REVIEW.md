## Decision and preserved controls

The precise manufacturer phrase registered as `CLAIM_PRODUCT_XTEND_PLUS_DESCRIPTION` is:

> Multi-immune food supplement with 22 naturally derived micro- and phytonutrients

The [official US page for SKU 302200](https://www.zinzino.com/shop/site/US/en-US/products/shop/302200), retrieved September 10, still displays that phrase. Provenance is confirmed; finished-product immune efficacy is **not substantiated by the evidence assembled here**. That conclusion is about this record, not proof that no relevant evidence exists.

Keep `ELEVATED / YELLOW / HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `MANUFACTURER_ONLY`, and `PRODUCT_SPECIFIC`. Keep the current exact-wording/manufacturer-attribution qualification, `DISCLOSURE_ZINZINO_PARTNER`, `DISCLOSURE_FDA_DSHEA_PLURAL`, and the requirement for a claim-adjacent disclaimer. Registry dates remain August 17/18, 2026, with the existing August 18, 2027 recheck date. This report's retrieval date does not replace them. No other inherited finding is resolved by this work.

## Analysis of the registered phrase

| Component | What this review can establish | Evidence gap and editorial disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product identity and supplement category | The manufacturer identifies the US SKU as Xtend+ and supplies the registered description. | Identity attribution is usable. It does not establish safety, personal need, or an outcome. |
| Immune implication | Nutrients participate in immune function; that is a biological rationale. | A benefit from this finished mixture, at the actual consumer dose, needs claim-matched evidence. Do not turn nutrient physiology into a claim that users will resist infection, recover faster, or have a superior immune response. Keep human review open. |
| Count of 22 | The count is a manufacturer assertion. | Obtain its counting method and controlling label; distinguish declared vitamins/minerals, plant mixtures, and individual compounds. This review does not independently verify a 22-item assay or batch composition. Do not convert the count into 22 proven benefits. |
| Natural derivation | The page identifies plant/mineral origins for selected ingredients. | Origin requires supply/formulation documentation; it does not establish better absorption, greater effectiveness, or lower risk than another source. Omit superiority and safety inferences. |
| Micro-/phytonutrient composition | This is a composition description. | A mixture of ingredients is not evidence of an additive or synergistic clinical effect. The evidence record has no population, comparator, or outcomes and expressly supports disclosed product facts only. |

The [NIH ODS immune-function fact sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ImmuneFunction-HealthProfessional/), updated March 10, 2025 and retrieved September 10, 2026, supports the distinction between nutrient adequacy and supplementation effects: inadequate nutrient status can impair immune function, while routine micronutrient supplementation without deficiency is unlikely to materially prevent or treat specific infections. It does not evaluate Xtend+.

## Adjacent manufacturer assertions screened, without importing them

The US page also presents increased energy, improved bone/joint function, tissue growth/repair, immune effects attributed to yeast beta-glucans, a food-equivalence comparison exceeding 3,000 kcal, a count exceeding 100 EFSA-associated benefits, and complementarity with other branded products. These are manufacturer assertions, not new Mindful Matrix claims. [US manufacturer page](https://www.zinzino.com/shop/site/US/en-US/products/shop/302200)

| Assertion category | Scientific support that would be needed | Finding for proposed website use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More energy | A defined outcome such as fatigue/function, in an identified population with stated baseline nutrient status, tested at the actual formulation/dose. | Metabolic participation does not establish that a nourished consumer feels more energetic. No suitable finished-product evidence was identified here. Do not import. |
| Better bone or joint function | Separate skeletal, muscle, pain, mobility, and fracture endpoints; evaluate each rather than combining them. | Bone-related nutrient biology cannot establish less joint pain or improved mobility. No finished-product outcome evidence was identified here. Do not import. |
| Growth/repair or comprehensive nutritional coverage | Defined nutrient requirements and clinical outcomes, accounting for diet and population. | No basis here for a universal completeness claim or replacement of varied food intake. Do not import. |
| Enhanced immunity from yeast beta-glucans | Match glucan preparation, purity/structure, dose, formulation, population, duration and prespecified endpoints. | Ingredient trials below offer limited, mixed, population-specific findings. They do not substantiate Xtend+ or establish infection prevention in ordinary consumers. |
| Food-calorie equivalence | Reproducible food set, nutrient targets, serving assumptions, composition database/version, and comparison calculation. | No reproducible calculation reviewed. Even a valid nutrient arithmetic comparison would not establish whole-food equivalence or clinical benefit. Do not import. |
| Numerous EFSA-associated benefits | Identify each exact authorized nutrient statement and its conditions, then separately assess US commercial use and the finished-product implication. | Counting ingredient statements cannot establish an equal number of clinical outcomes for a mixture. No independent product endorsement is established. |
| Complete branded combination program | Direct comparison of the actual combination with an appropriate control, including potential harms and nutrient overlap. | No direct combination trial was identified in this screen. Do not infer necessity, synergy or completeness. |
| Certifications and dietary suitability | Verify current certificate, scope, applicable product/batch and certification body's record. | A quality-management, vegan, religious-dietary or selected-substance-testing certificate does not demonstrate immune efficacy. No certificate revalidation was performed. |

These are editorial judgments about evidence fit, not a legal determination. The [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance), issued December 2022 and retrieved September 10, 2026, calls for substantiation of express and implied health messages in their overall presentation. Manufacturer attribution and a disclaimer cannot supply missing evidence. A reviewer should assess product summaries, discovery/search, social metadata and Product structured data together.

## Ingredient trials: what the evidence does and does not transfer

All records below were retrieved September 10, 2026. These are primary study records/abstracts, not a completed full-text systematic review. Trial doses are research descriptions, not recommendations.

| Primary source | Population, intervention, comparator, duration and outcome | Limits and transfer decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Habibullah et al., iScience 2026; PMID 41883579](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41883579/), DOI 10.1016/j.isci.2026.115120; published February 25, 2026 | Randomized, double-blind trial in 198 moderately stressed adults; yeast beta-glucan 120 or 204 mg/day versus placebo for 12 weeks. Primary endpoint: WURSS-21 respiratory symptom severity. The abstract reports adjusted mean changes of +3.42 for placebo, -2.27 at 120 mg and -3.51 at 204 mg; only the higher dose exceeded the authors' clinical-importance threshold. | Symptom scores in a selected population are not laboratory-confirmed infection prevention or Xtend+ outcomes. Verify analysis population, between-group contrasts, missingness, threshold and multiplicity in full text. PubMed states no conflicts; one affiliation is RB (Health) Malaysia. Full funding/role review remains pending. PMC returned a browser challenge; it was not bypassed. |
| [Fuller et al., Nutrition 2012; PMID 22465901](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22465901/), DOI 10.1016/j.nut.2011.11.012 | 100 healthy adults, mean age about 21; Wellmune 250 mg/day versus rice-flour placebo for 90 days; 97 completed. Total symptomatic days were 198 versus 241, with P=.06. One breathing score differed; other severity scores did not. | A nonsignificant total-days result and selected secondary findings cannot become broad proof of prevention. Complete funding, prespecification and multiplicity assessment remain pending. This ingredient/preparation is not established as formulation-equivalent to the US product. |
| [Mah et al., Journal of Medicinal Food 2020; PMID 31573387](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31573387/), DOI 10.1089/jmf.2019.0076; online October 1, 2019 | Marathon runners consumed dairy beverages with 250 mg/day insoluble glucan (69), soluble glucan (76), or placebo (133) for 91 days around a marathon. The abstract reports reduced total symptom severity for insoluble glucan; soluble glucan did not differ from placebo. | Physical form mattered. Exercise stress, beverage matrix and symptom reporting limit generalization. Related beverage publications must not be counted as independent replication without cohort checks. Full report/funding review is pending. Neither arm tested Xtend+. |

The newer positive ingredient result is included to avoid a one-sided account. It still cannot bridge an unknown finished-product dose/formulation. The screen does not establish that all beta-glucans are ineffective, nor that all preparations share the reported effects.

[Previously inspected US sheet](https://zinzinowebstorage.blob.core.windows.net/product-sheets/Xtend-plus-en-US.pdf)

Do not silently reconcile those documents. Obtain the controlling current US label and formulation, its revision date, complete directions/warnings, and the reason for the differences. Dose-specific evidence cannot simply transfer across a twofold capsule range. No personalized safety assurance is possible from the material assembled here.
