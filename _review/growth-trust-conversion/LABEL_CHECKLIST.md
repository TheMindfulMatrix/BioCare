# A clearer way to read a supplement label

NON-PUBLIC RESOURCE DRAFT — prepared 2026-09-06 for editorial review. Not a new published Library guide, not sent by email, and not gated behind a signup.

## Reader checklist

1. **Identify the exact product.** Check the name, manufacturer, form and label version. A similar name does not establish the same formulation.
2. **Find the serving size.** Distinguish one capsule, scoop or tablet from a full labeled serving.
3. **Read amounts per serving.** Keep the amount and unit together. Do not fill in missing quantities from another product.
4. **Separate the label from the headline.** The ingredient list and the advertised outcome answer different questions.
5. **Look for limitations.** Note cautions, directions, allergen information and anything the label leaves unclear.
6. **Check the source.** Prefer the current manufacturer label for product identity. Use the Library's references to investigate broader evidence rather than treating the label as an independent clinical study.
7. **Write down your unresolved question.** Ask the manufacturer for missing product information. Discuss personal suitability and medication questions with a qualified clinician or pharmacist.
8. **Keep the buying decision separate.** Check the current total, purchase format, recurring charges and terms before choosing. Reading does not obligate you to buy.

Continue with the existing public guide: [How to read a supplement label](https://themindfulmatrixhealth.com/library/how-to-read-a-supplement-label.html). Its canonical source is `content/library.json`, article `how-to-read-a-supplement-label`.

This checklist organizes questions; it does not provide a dosing plan or individual medical advice. Some Matrix product links are partner links and may result in compensation. Those relationships do not establish product suitability.
