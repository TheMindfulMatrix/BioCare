A simple way forward.

You do not need every answer today. Begin with the stage that best matches where you are now.

  1. 01

    Information

    Understand your situation.

    Start with the question, pattern, or measurement you actually have.

    What do I know right now?
  2. 02

    Education

    Learn what it means.

    Use credible sources, context, and limitations to separate what is known from what remains uncertain.

    What does this information support?
  3. 03

    Action

    Decide what—if anything—to do next.

    Action might mean learning more, changing a habit, asking a better question, discussing it with a healthcare professional, measuring when appropriate, doing nothing yet, or considering a tool for a clear reason.

    What is the next useful step?
Action does not automatically mean buying something. A tool or product is only one possible option when there is a clear reason for it.This page is an orientation, not a diagnostic.
