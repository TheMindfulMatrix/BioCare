A free sample · Original planning exercise

Start with one repeatable action

Choose one ordinary situation: breakfast, arriving home, or preparing for bed. Attach a small action to it and write a fallback for difficult days. This is an exercise in planning, not a promise that a habit will form in a fixed number of days. Track whether the action fit your schedule, not whether you were a good or bad person. If the plan repeatedly fails, shorten it or change the cue. Do not add several supplements, a strict diet, and a new training plan at once; that makes the experience harder to understand.

Try it on paper

Write: after ___, I will ___. On a difficult day I will ___. Review after seven days and change one element.

You miss three days. What comes next?

Resume with a smaller, realistic action; do not punish yourself or double the workload.

Keep notes privately. This website does not collect your habit records, medical history, test results, or course answers.
