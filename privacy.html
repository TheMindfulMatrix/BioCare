Privacy & website data

Know what happens when you browse.

This notice describes the current static website. Optional analytics and newsletter registration are not enabled in this version.

Notice date: 2026-09-06 · Analytics off · Newsletter signup off

Browsing and search

The site does not ask you to create an account. Search and filters run in your browser. Search words and filter selections can appear in the page address and browser history, so do not enter sensitive personal information.

The site does not currently send analytics events, record browsing sessions, or store advertising identifiers. Ordinary website delivery is different: GitHub Pages and the network services involved may process connection information such as your IP address and the requested address.

Fonts and external websites

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts that service to obtain the files. Product and social links lead to other websites with their own data practices.

Affiliate identifiers in product links identify the partner relationship. The manufacturer operates checkout; this website does not collect your payment details or receive the contents of your test report through a website form.

If you email us

Opening an email link starts your email app; it does not subscribe you to marketing. If you send a message, your email address and message contents are handled through the email services involved, including our Microsoft 365 mailbox, to respond to your inquiry.

Only include what is needed for your question. Please do not send health records, test results, financial details, or passwords. Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, or retention of correspondence. Retention can depend on the correspondence and applicable service settings; no automatic deletion period is represented here.

Optional services are not active

There is no active newsletter signup on the public site. No email address is required to read the available guides. If a mailing service or analytics service is introduced, its purpose, data handling, and applicable choices must be explained before activation.
