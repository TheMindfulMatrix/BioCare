TEST → MEASURE → ACT → RETEST

A responsible measurement journey.

  1. 01 / Test

    Begin with a clear question.

    Selected fatty acids can be measured in blood. The specimen, laboratory method, units, and collection context matter.

  2. 02 / Measure

    Understand what comes back.

    A result reports selected fatty acids in the analyzed specimen. Red-blood-cell measures reflect a longer intake window than plasma or serum.

  3. 03 / Act

    Keep the conclusion proportional.

    Use the reported measurement only for the question it can support. A reasonable next step may be learning, discussing the result, changing a habit, or doing nothing yet.

  4. 04 / Retest

    Compare like with like.

    Comparable repeat testing can document biomarker change. It does not by itself establish a broader outcome, and this journey prescribes no universal retest interval.

Evidence and limitations

Follow the evidence past the number.

The evidence-reviewed Library guides document sources from NIH, peer-reviewed methodological research, randomized trials, and an interlaboratory comparison. They explain specimen differences, response variability, laboratory comparability, and the limits of universal thresholds.
