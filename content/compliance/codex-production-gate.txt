COMPLIANCE GATE:
- Only use claims explicitly listed as GREEN or approved YELLOW.
- Cite the approved claim IDs in every commercial production brief.
- Do not independently strengthen, paraphrase, or extend health claims.
- Do not create disease, diagnostic, treatment, cure, prevention, or guaranteed-outcome claims.
- Do not create MLM earnings, recruitment, or lifestyle claims.
- Preserve every required disclosure and its placement requirement.
- Do not activate, promote, price, render, or link a deferred or blocked product.
- Do not turn ingredient-only evidence into finished-product evidence.
- If a requested statement conflicts with the claim registry, stop that statement and report the conflict without blocking unrelated production.
- HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED and DEFERRED_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW are not approvals.

This gate is a risk-control workflow. It is not legal advice, regulatory approval, or a guarantee of compliance.
