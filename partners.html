Original course in development

Partner Practice

In development · Not available for purchase. Course outlines and a free sample are available here; enrollment and checkout are not open.

The curriculum focuses on skills and informed decisions, with no earnings or lifestyle promises. It is separate from the everyday-habits course.

  1. Understand the independent business

    Describe responsibilities and risks before deciding to participate.

  2. Budget and records before ambition

    Distinguish revenue, expenses, and a spending limit.

  3. Learn products without practising medicine

    Explain catalog facts while respecting evidence boundaries.

  4. Marketing people can evaluate

    Plan helpful messages with clear commercial context.

  5. Networking through permission

    Have useful conversations without pressure.

  6. Events with a clear purpose

    Prepare a small, accessible learning event.

  7. Speaking with evidence and humility

    Deliver a concise talk without exaggeration.

  8. Teams and Zoom meetings

    Host a useful meeting with basic privacy and accessibility.

  9. Support a team without pressure

    Create a fair, repeatable support routine.

  10. A measured 30-day business experiment

    Decide whether to continue using actual evidence.

Start with a few honest questions.

  • What are my actual obligations, recurring costs, and exit options?
  • Can I explain the product facts and their limits without making medical claims?
  • Do I have a realistic time and spending limit?
  • Can people say no without pressure or repeated messages?

Keep financial, customer, and health records private. This website does not collect applications or host team meetings.
